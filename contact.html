Prospective students & postdocs

I'm always glad to hear from students excited about the early universe. Prospective graduate students should apply to the UT Austin Department of Astronomy graduate program and are welcome to get in touch to learn about current projects.

Interested undergraduates at UT Austin can join our research through the Galaxy Evolution VIP program. To see who you'd be working with, meet the group.
